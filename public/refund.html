This policy applies to every purchase on Easy Wedding and is written to follow the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. All prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

1. What you buy and how it is delivered

Easy Wedding sells digital services only: an invitation design (or the All-Designs Wedding Pass) and optional add-ons such as a Hindi version, PDF, photo gallery or short link. Nothing is shipped. There is no physical delivery and no shipping charge. After your payment is confirmed, your account is created or updated and the design unlocks immediately in your dashboard, where you can edit and share it. If it does not unlock, see “Failed or double payments” below.

2. Preview before you pay

Every design can be previewed in full for free with sample names. Please preview on your phone and read what is included before you buy. Because the preview is free and complete, we cannot refund a purchase just because you changed your mind about the design.

3. Cancellation

  • Before payment: you can leave at any time. Nothing is charged and no account is created until your payment succeeds.
  • After the design has unlocked: the service has been delivered and cannot be cancelled or returned. See section 4 for the cases in which we refund.
  • Closing your account: you can ask us to delete your account at any time (see our Privacy policy). Closing an account does not by itself entitle you to a refund.

4. When we refund in full

  • You were charged twice for the same design or add-on.
  • Money was debited but the design did not unlock, and we cannot fix it within 48 hours.
  • A technical fault on our side stops your invitation from working and we cannot fix it.
  • We are unable to provide the service you paid for for any other reason that is our fault.

If you were charged an amount different from the price shown to you at checkout, we will refund the difference.

5. When a refund is not available

  • You changed your mind after the design unlocked, or prefer another design (the preview is free). If a design you bought is not what you need, contact us — we will try to help, for example with an add-on or adjustment, but this is at our discretion.
  • A mistake in content you entered (spelling, date, venue) — you can edit your invitation as often as you like.
  • Problems caused by your device, browser, internet connection or by the way a guest’s phone shows the page.
  • Misuse of the service, or content we remove because it breaks our Terms of service.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after payment, except where the law gives you a longer period.

6. How to ask for a refund

Message us on WhatsApp +91 87899 91983 or call +91 87899 91983 (10 AM to 8 PM) within 7 days of payment and give your account email and the payment reference (shown on your payment confirmation or bank/UPI statement). We may ask for a screenshot of the debit message. We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and tell you the decision.

8. Failed or double payments

If your payment fails or is stuck in “pending”, do not pay again straight away. Banks and payment gateways usually reverse a failed debit automatically within 5–7 working days. If the amount is not returned by then, or if the design did not unlock after a successful payment, contact us with the payment reference and we will check with the gateway and fix or refund it.

9. Coupons and offers

A refund returns the amount you actually paid, after any coupon. Coupon value and promotional credits are not paid out in cash. Launch and season offers apply for a limited period; a price change never affects a design you have already unlocked.

10. If you are not satisfied

Write to our Grievance Officer, Chandan Kumar Mahato, on WhatsApp +91 87899 91983 — we reply within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. You may also approach the National Consumer Helpline (1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in) or your District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (e-Daakhil) at any time. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under Indian consumer law.
