Help center & FAQ

A little clarity.

Is Easy Wedding free?

Planning is free — the workspace, budget, checklist and all 11 invitation previews cost nothing. You pay only when you publish your own invitation: at Grand Opening prices a single design is ₹399–₹799 (regular ₹499–₹999), and the All-Designs Pass is ₹1,199 (regular ₹1,499).

How do I pay?

When you create your invitation link in the studio, pick a payment option and pay with UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps), a debit or credit card, or netbanking. Your design unlocks the moment your payment succeeds.

What do I get after paying?

Your chosen design unlocks instantly. Create your shareable invitation link and QR code with your own photos, words and wedding song. One payment covers one design for one wedding — the All-Designs Pass covers all 11.

Can I edit my invitation after paying?

Yes. Edit your details any time and create a fresh link — each link holds a snapshot of your details, so make a new link after editing. Your unlocked design stays yours.

How do guests open invitations?

Share your invitation link or QR code. Guests tap to open it on any phone — no app to install and no sign-in needed.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Plan in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer — and your guests open invitations without any app.

Are there any extra charges?

No hidden charges. The design price covers your invitation link and QR code for your whole wedding. Optional extras — personal links for each guest, a Hindi version, a printable PDF, a larger photo gallery and a short link — are priced separately in the studio, from ₹99.

Can I get a refund?

See our . If a payment went through but your design did not unlock, contact us and we will fix it the same day.

Where is my plan stored?

Your wedding plan is saved to your Easy Wedding account, so it is there on every phone and computer you sign in on. You can also use Download backup in your wedding profile to keep a copy of your own.

Can I try it first?

Open the live demo to explore a complete sample wedding.
