This policy explains how Easy Wedding (easywedding.in, owned and operated by Chandan Kumar Mahato) handles personal data. It is written to follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it (including the Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information Rules, 2011), and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

1. Who we are

Easy Wedding lets couples create animated digital wedding invitations and plan their wedding (events, budget, checklist, vendors, outfits). For the data of couples who create an account, Easy Wedding is the data fiduciary under the DPDP Act. You can reach us at WhatsApp +91 87899 91983 or +91 87899 91983 (10 AM to 8 PM, every day).

3. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account, save your plan and photographs, and publish and share your invitation.
  • To take payment, unlock the design you bought, issue invoices or receipts and handle refunds.
  • To help you with support requests, password recovery and service messages about your order.
  • To keep the service safe: preventing fraud, abuse, repeated login attempts and unauthorised access.
  • To improve the website using anonymous, aggregated statistics.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Our legal basis is your consent (given when you create an account and accept this policy) and, where the DPDP Act allows, legitimate uses such as performing the service you asked for and complying with the law. We do not use your wedding details or photographs for advertising, we do not sell personal data, and we do not train AI models on your photographs.

4. Guests and RSVP replies

When a guest replies to an invitation, their name, reply and any phone number or message they type are sent only to the couple who invited them. For that information the couple decides what to ask and why; Easy Wedding only provides the tool and stores it on the couple’s behalf. We do not use guest details for marketing, we do not share or sell them, and we never message guests. A couple can delete replies at any time. Couples must collect guest details lawfully and only for the wedding.

We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone. If the business is ever transferred, your data would move only to a successor bound by this policy.

Some of our providers run servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside India. The DPDP Act permits this except to countries the Government of India restricts, and we use providers that apply recognised security standards.

6. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes. Your mobile number is used for payment and for password-recovery checks; a protected (hashed) form is used for that check.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Uploaded photographs are kept in private storage and are shown only through your account or a link you choose to share.
  • Access to administration tools is limited to the owner and protected by a separate login.
  • Failed-login limits and monitoring help block misuse.

No online service can promise perfect security. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, wedding details and photographs: for as long as your account is active, and until you ask us to delete them.
  • Payment and invoice records: for the period Indian tax and accounting laws require, even after you delete your account.
  • Support messages: only as long as needed to resolve the issue and keep a record of it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: kept only in aggregated form, without your name or contact details.
  • Guest RSVP replies: until the couple deletes them or the account is deleted.

When you delete your account or ask us to, we erase or irreversibly anonymise your personal data, except what the law requires us to keep. Backups are overwritten in the normal course within about 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or complete inaccurate data, and update it;
  • ask us to erase your personal data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what was done before, and some services may then stop working);
  • have your grievance addressed, and to nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot do so yourself.

You can edit most details yourself in the dashboard under My profile. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp +91 87899 91983 from the number on your account. We will confirm your identity, then act and reply within the time the law allows. If you are not satisfied with our reply you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Login cookie (essential): keeps you signed in securely. It is HttpOnly and cannot be read by page scripts.
  • Browser storage (essential or convenience): a random visitor ID for our own anonymous statistics, your motion on/off preference and draft invitation text so you do not lose work.
  • We use no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking and no third-party analytics or advertising pixels.

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time; you will simply be signed out.

10. Children

Easy Wedding is meant for adults planning a wedding. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or pay. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a child’s data has reached us, tell us and we will delete it. Photographs of minor family members that a couple uploads are the couple’s responsibility and must be shared with the consent of the child’s parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or the law changes. The effective date at the top always shows the current version. If a change is important, we will tell account holders (for example by a notice on the dashboard).
