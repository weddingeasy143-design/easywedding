Questions couples ask

Can I see my invitation before I pay?

Yes. Tap ▶ on any design to play its live preview. When you choose a design, you fill in your details and watch a live preview as you type. You pay only at the end, when you are happy with it.

How do I pay?

With UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps), a debit or credit card, or netbanking. Your design unlocks the moment your payment succeeds.

What do I get after paying?

Your own invitation link and QR code to share on WhatsApp, and a dashboard to manage your wedding. One payment covers one design for your whole wedding — every function in it.

How do guests open the invitation?

They tap the link or scan the QR code. It opens on any phone — no app to install and no sign-in needed.

Can I change details after paying?

Yes. Log in any time to update names, dates, venues, photos or the song from your dashboard.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The price on the design is what you pay for it. The All-Designs Pass unlocks all 11 designs for one wedding, so you can use a different design for every function.
