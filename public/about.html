About Easy Wedding

Plan less. Celebrate more.

What is Easy Wedding?

Easy Wedding is a simple website for Indian couples and families to create a beautiful animated wedding invitation and plan the whole wedding in one place — without spreadsheets, screenshots and endless WhatsApp forwards.

What you can do

  • Invitations: choose from 11 animated designs, add your names, photographs, venue and events, and share one link with every guest on WhatsApp. Guests need no app.
  • Planner: keep Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding and Reception together with dates, venues and dress codes.
  • Budget: track every expense in rupees, with contingency, and export a printable plan.
  • Checklist, vendors and outfits: keep tasks, vendor quotes and bookings, outfit ideas and shopping lists in one place.

How it works

  • Preview every design free.
  • Pick your design and pay once; your account and login are ready straight away.
  • Add your details and photographs, preview, and share your invitation.

Who is behind it

Easy Wedding is created, owned and run by Chandan Kumar Mahato from Dhanbad, Jharkhand, India. It is built for how Indian weddings really work — many functions, many families, a lot of details — and priced simply, in rupees, with UPI and card payments.

What we care about

  • Simple: few buttons, plain words, works on any phone.
  • Private: your plan and photographs are yours. We never sell your data or show ads (see our Privacy policy).
  • Fair: clear prices, a free preview, and a written refund policy.
  • Original: every design and animation is made for Easy Wedding (see Copyright).
